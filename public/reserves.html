How to read it

  1. Held is the USDG balance of the treasury address, read straight from Robinhood Chain.
  2. Owed is everything players could take out right now: USDG balances, model tokens at their exit value, and withdrawals not yet paid.
  3. If held ever falls below owed, withdrawals pause on their own until it is fixed. Balances stay safe while they are paused.
